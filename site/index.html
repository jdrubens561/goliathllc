My Story

After more than 20 years leading operations across Fortune 500 companies, third-party logistics providers, and family-owned businesses, I learned one simple truth: change isn't difficult because organizations lack ideas—it fails because of execution. Too often, consulting engagements end with a polished presentation, a strategic roadmap, and a final invoice—but little support for the difficult work of implementation. Having experienced this as a Manager, Director, and Vice President, I saw a niche in the market to create real change. I formed Goliath Consulting LLC, a unique service provider breaking the industry model.

I believe meaningful transformation happens through collaboration, accountability, and execution—not just recommendations.

Supply Chain Logistics

An efficient supply chain is more than a competitive advantage — it’s the foundation of a resilient business. At Goliath, we partner with organizations to optimize at every stage of the chain.

Either bring us your current supply chain concern or let us review the total process. We can provide analysis and solutions on multiple levels. Below are a few of our key points:

Suppliers:

  • What resiliency do your suppliers have?
  • What backup models are available in case of disruption?

Warehousing/Inventory Control:

  • Is your product flow optimal?
  • Does your footprint match your needs?
  • Do you have the right tools to move product through the operation?
  • Does the inventory days of supply match your current volume?
  • What stratification of items are placed to match velocity?

Transportation:

  • What modes are most efficient for your business?
  • Do you have visibility of goods from supplier to end customer?

Operational Efficiency

Every organization has opportunity to improve —but lasting operational excellent isn’t achieved through quick fixes or across-the-board cost reductions. It’s built by understanding how people, processes and technology work together to create value.

We know that one of your greatest resources is the people who know your operation best. Rather than focusing on headcount reduction we partner with your front line teams to understand their workflow. Some of the most cost effective solutions come from those that live your operation daily.

Part of our process:

Interview floor operations and critical office staff.
Generate reporting, analytics based on the team’s feedback.
Evaluate the most cost efficient projects with ROI
Work with senior leadership and production floor management to implement.
Lasting support from implementation to completion so that you see the results.

Customer Intelligence & Engagement

Your customers critical resources for business insight. Understanding what they think, expect and experience is essential to building stronger relationships, improving products and services, and maintaining a competitive advantage.

At Goliath, we help organizations transform customer feedback into meaningful business intelligence. By leveraging advanced analytic and AI-powered tools, we gather and interpret insights from customer surveys, online reviews, social media, industry forums, and other other digital channels. This helps identify emerging trends, opportunities, and potential risks.

Data along doesn’t drive better decision; actionable insights do. We work with your team to develop reporting that delivers clear, relevant information to every level of the organization. This ensures leaders have the visibility they need to respond quickly, improve the customer experience, and make informed strategic decision.

When your organization understands the voice of the customer, every decision becomes more intentional, responsive, and aligned with long-term success.

Executive Partnership & Change Leadership

Successful transformation requires more than great ideas—it requires committed leadership. Lasting change happens when executives provide clear direction, remove barriers, and remain actively engaged throughout the implementation process.

Having served in leadership roles from Manager to Vice President, we understand the competing priorities executives face every day. That's why Goliath Consulting operates as an extension of your leadership team, providing the transparency and accountability needed to keep initiatives moving forward without creating unnecessary administrative burden.

We establish clear performance metrics, monitor progress, and deliver concise executive updates that highlight accomplishments, risks, and next steps. Rather than lengthy status meetings, our focus is on providing timely, actionable information that enables leaders to make informed decisions quickly and confidently.

By aligning executive leadership with frontline teams, we help ensure that strategic initiatives don't lose momentum after the planning phase, they become lasting improvements embedded throughout the organization.

Business Resilience and Risk Management

Every business faces operational and supply chain risks, but organizations that proactively identify and manage those risks are better positioned to maintain performance, protect profitability, and respond confidently to change. We partner with you to evaluate vulnerabilities across your supply chain, operations, inventory, supplier network, and business processes, transforming potential risks into actionable improvement opportunities.

Our approach goes beyond identifying issues; we provide practical, data driven solutions that strengthen resilience while supporting your operational goals. By developing customized risk mitigation strategies and implementation plans, we help reduce disruptions, improve decision-making, and create a more agile organization prepared for future challenges.

Deliverables Include:

Comprehensive operational and supply chain risk assessment
Risk heat map with prioritized areas of concern
Supplier risk evaluation and mitigation recommendations
Business continuity and contingency planning
Process vulnerability analysis with improvement recommendations
Inventory and capacity risk assessment
Actionable risk mitigation roadmap with implementation priorities
Executive summary and presentation of findings with strategic recommendations
